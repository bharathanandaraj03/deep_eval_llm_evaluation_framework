## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year; any additional unused days are forfeited.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
